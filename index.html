Who We Are

Chemistry of Atmospheric Pollutants Research Laboratory

We are a research group at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, dedicated to understanding the chemical composition, formation mechanisms, and transformation pathways of atmospheric pollutants — from individual molecules to city-scale pollution episodes.

Our research spans secondary aerosol formation, aerosol optical characterisation, indoor air quality, fog–aerosol interactions, tropospheric ozone chemistry, and long-term trends of aerosols in the atmosphere. We combine field measurements, laboratory experiments, satellite data, and atmospheric modelling to build a comprehensive picture of air pollution over India.

Our findings directly support evidence-based environmental policy, public health assessment, and sustainable urban planning. Reach us at caps.iitb@gmail.com.

Lab Leadership

Principal Investigator

Principal Investigator · Core Faculty
Prof. Abhishek Chakraborty
Assistant Professor · Environmental Science & Engineering Department, IIT Bombay

Prof. Chakraborty leads the Chemistry of Atmospheric Pollutants (CAP) Laboratory at IIT Bombay (Room ES 408, ESED-DESE Building). His doctoral work at IIT Kanpur focused on fog and organic aerosol interactions — their sources, composition, and chemistry. His research interests include atmospheric processing of organic aerosols, fog–smog–aerosol interactions, physicochemical characterisation and source apportionment of aerosols, real-time air pollution monitoring, and air pollution control technologies. He has been cited over 1,100 times and is an awardee of the Academic Excellence Award, IIT Kanpur.

What We Study

Research Areas

Our work spans the full lifecycle of air pollutants — from emission to deposition.

🌫️

Secondary Aerosol Formation

Investigating formation pathways of secondary organic aerosols (SOA) and secondary inorganic aerosols from VOCs, NOₓ, and SO₂ under varying atmospheric conditions.

🔭

Aerosol Optical Properties

Quantifying scattering, absorption, and extinction coefficients of aerosols and understanding their role in radiative forcing and climate.

🌡️

Heatwaves & Tropospheric Ozone

Studying the interplay between extreme heat events, wildfire emissions, and tropospheric ozone chemistry.

🏠

Indoor Air Quality & Health

Characterising indoor aerosol chemistry and its health impact using GIS-based spatial exposure analysis.

📡

High Pollution Episodes

Ground-reanalysis of CPCB–CAMS data, secondary aerosol formation chemistry, and statistical air quality analysis.

🔗

Emission & Air Quality Linkages

Source–receptor relationships between emission inventories and observed air quality trends across Indian cities.

🧪

Organo Nitrates & Organo Sulfates

Characterisation of organo nitrates and sulfates as key atmospheric oxidation products contributing to aerosol mass.

💨

Air Quality & Exposure Assessment

Population exposure assessment using atmospheric chemistry models, aerosol science, and air pollution modelling.

💧

Aqueous Processing & Fog–Aerosol

Aqueous-phase chemistry in fog droplets and how fog–aerosol interactions alter particle chemical composition.

📏

Size-Resolved Aerosol Formation

Size-segregated analysis of secondary aerosol formation — reactivity, lifetime, deposition, and health impacts.

📈

Long-Term Aerosol Trends

Multi-year datasets identifying trends in aerosol loading, chemical composition, and optical properties across India.

🌦️

Meteorological Influence on Dispersion

Boundary layer dynamics, monsoon meteorology, and temperature inversions controlling pollutant accumulation.

Infrastructure

Lab Facilities & Instruments

State-of-the-art analytical instruments for atmospheric research.

⚗️ ACSM

Near-real-time measurements of chemical composition and mass concentrations of non-refractory submicron particulate matter.

🌫️ CASS

Measurement and characterisation of carbonaceous aerosol components — organic carbon (OC) and elemental carbon (EC).

🔥 OC-EC Analyzer

Thermal–optical analysis to measure organic carbon (OC) and elemental carbon (EC) in particulate matter.

🔦 Aethalometer (AE36)

Real-time multi-wavelength measurement of black carbon and brown carbon with source apportionment capability.

👁️ Visibility Sensor

Measures atmospheric visibility by detecting light scattering caused by airborne particles and aerosols.

🌤️ Automatic Weather Station

Continuous recording of temperature, humidity, wind speed, wind direction, rainfall, and atmospheric pressure.
